`public/audio/companion.wav` and `companion.json` (her sighs, hmms, laughs and so on) are cut from these by `tools/slice_companion_audio.py`, using Freesound's high-quality MP3 previews. `public/audio/companion-voice.mp3` / `.json` (Amy's spoken lines) and `companion-voice-jess.mp3` / `.json` (Jess's) are generated by `tools/generate_companion_voice.py` with Chatterbox (https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox, MIT licence), which voices each line from a short reference recording in `tools/voices/`:

- `amy-ref.wav`: twelve seconds of the Piper text-to-speech engine's `en_GB-cori-high` voice (https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori), whose model card gives its licence as public domain: it was trained on LibriVox recordings. Amy's lines were first recorded with Piper itself.
- `jess-ref.wav`: twelve seconds of Ruth Golding reading in RP for LibriVox's "A Celebration of Dialects and Accents, Vol. 1" (https://archive.org/details/dialect_accent_0909_librivox, track 4), released into the public domain.

The generated recordings are released CC0 like the rest of the project's own audio. Chatterbox adds Resemble AI's inaudible Perth watermark marking the audio as machine-generated. Chatterbox, Piper (GPL-3.0) and Whisper (used to check each line, MIT) are only tools for making the recordings and don't ship with the game.

## Modifications

- `companion.glb` is recoloured at load time (navy polo, green wellies) by swapping entries in its palette texture, and gets a collar, polo placket and welly shafts built from primitives and attached to her bones. See `src/npc/outfit.js`. The file itself is unchanged.

## Built in code

These have no external source:

- **Quad bike**: primitives in `src/vehicle/quadModel.js`. No CC0 quad or ATV model was available; Poly Pizza only had CC-BY motorbikes and buggies. The model is one group with named parts (`body`, `handlebars`, `wheel_fl` / `wheel_fr` / `wheel_rl` / `wheel_rr`, `seat`, `pillionSeat`) so it can be swapped for a real model later.
- **Round bales** (straw and black-wrapped silage) and the **five-bar field gate**: primitives in `src/world/props.js`, with canvas-drawn straw textures.
- **Jerry can** and **petrol tank**: primitives in `src/items/`.
- **Quad body styles** (utility, utility with brush guard, two-stroke sport): all primitives in `src/vehicle/quadModel.js`.
- **The Land Rover**: primitives in `src/vehicle/landRoverModel.js`, with its Series dash drawn on canvas in `src/vehicle/dash.js`.
- **Dashes**: drawn on canvas in `src/vehicle/dash.js`.
- **Terrain, track, grass tufts, distant trees and sky**: generated in `src/world/`.
- **Synthesised engine sound** (selectable in the tuning panel), relay clicks, fuel pump, pouring and wind: synthesised at runtime in `src/audio/`.

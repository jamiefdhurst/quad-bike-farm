Size

Keeping it smooth at this size

  • Ground in tiles. Fine near you, coarse far off, and only the tiles in view are drawn.
  • Hedges with a far version. Kilometres of hedge become a cheap strip beyond about 150 m.
  • Distance cut-offs as now: set pieces, small parts, shadows and ground cover drop out with distance, and haze hides the edge.
  • Built as you need it. Near tiles first behind the start screen, the rest during play.
  • Held in CI. More budget spots in the smoke test, so a heavy corner fails a PR.

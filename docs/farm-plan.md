# The farm plan

Where everything on the farm goes and what each place is for, and the order the work is done in. `docs/what-the-game-is.md` says what each part of the game is for; this says where it is. A new thing you'd see in the world is placed on this plan before it is built: if no place fits, the plan changes first, in its own PR.

Settled with Jamie on 7 November 2026 and extended on 8 November. The map is `docs/farm-plan.svg`, drawn by `docs/farm-plan-map.html` (open it in a browser to click any place for what it is for) and rendered with `node tools/farmPlanSvg.mjs`. These files are the plan: nothing outside the repo is.

## The farm

A mixed upland-edge farm of about 68 ha (870 by 830 m), about seven times today's: sheep on the hill, suckler cattle on the river meadow, six arable fields in rotation, grass for hay and silage. The farmstead is in the middle at the head of a drive off the lane. The neighbour and Nether Thornby are across the lane. Back Lane, an old single-track road, loops round the farm's north, west and south, and crosses the Moor, the open common beyond it where the flock summers. One A-road runs down the valley to the east; beyond every edge is far landscape you see but never reach. Field names are dealt per farm from the `farmSeed`; the names below are examples.

## Rules the plan keeps

- **Daily walks stay short.** Everything the girls walk to every day is within about 80 m of the yard, however big the fields get.
- **One job per field.** A job happens on the field whose job it is (hay on the hay meadow, spraying on whichever crop needs it), never on a patch of a field that does something else.
- **Places are on the plan before they're built.** An issue that puts something in the world names its place here. If none fits, change the plan first.
- **No invisible walls.** Every way off the map turns you round where it leaves: at the A-road's junctions (the lane north, the village street), where the lane runs on south out of the valley towards Haddon, and at the ladder stile in the ridge wall. Each road visibly carries on into the far landscape. A field edge is a hedge, wall, fence or gate.
- **Boundaries say what they keep in.** Hedges round the arable, drystone walls on the hill and the sheep field, post-and-rail round Home Ground, wire where stock graze.
- **Every field has a landmark** of its own (a lone oak, old gateposts, a roller in the corner), and something tall is seen from every gate: the church tower, the Dutch barn's roof, the trig pillar, the power line.
- **Every place names its sound**: what you hear there (birds, water, the road, stock, wind, the church bells).
- **The animals have a year as the crops do**: where the flock and the herd are, and what needs doing, follows the calendar.
- **We see far and never see the edge.** On a clear day the view reaches about 1.5 km; beyond every edge is far landscape. The view distance is a setting.
- **The frame holds at any size.** The ground is drawn in tiles, fine near you and coarse far off; hedges and set pieces have far versions; every area has a budget spot in the smoke test.

## The phases

Each phase is a GitHub milestone, kept in `.github/settings.yml`. `/todo` takes a `🔜 next` issue first, then the lowest-numbered issue in the earliest open milestone whose `Depends on:` line names only closed issues. Work that doesn't depend on where things are (the girls, the vehicles, the engine, sound, items) is in the **Alongside** milestone and is taken with `/todo alongside` or by number. An issue that puts something in the world isn't started before its phase.

## How work stays joined up

- **Milestones** decide the order, not issue numbers.
- **Every issue has a "Where it fits" section**: its place on this plan (or "not placed"), and the systems it ties into: the girls, jobs and money, the save, the map, and how a player finds it. `/idea` writes it and `/todo` reads it in its plan step.
- **`/idea` checks this plan** for anything placed in the world, and puts each new issue in a milestone.
- **`/tidy check` compares the layout with this plan** and files what has drifted.
- **This plan changes in its own PR**, with a line in `docs/decisions/world.md`, before work that needs the change. The PR changes this file and the map page's numbers together and re-renders `docs/farm-plan.svg`.

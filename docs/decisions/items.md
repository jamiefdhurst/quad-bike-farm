## Toolbox (2026-10-05)

- Request: a way to tweak the idle and other things with the engine off, maybe with a toolbox, and ideally make the quads a bit faster. A toolbox you carry to a switched-off quad opens a panel with five real adjustments: idle screw, carb mixture, throttle stop (the speed limiter), clutch springs and tyre pressures (`src/vehicle/tuneUp.js`, pure and tested; panel in `src/workshop/`).
- Every setting is applied from a snapshot of the quad as you first found it, so "Back to stock" is exact. Safe limits are in the logic: the idle never goes below the spec's safe minimum (a lean mixture's idle drop is wound back in on the screw), the clutch always bites at least 400 rpm above idle, and the idle can't go higher than the stiffest clutch can clear.
- Limiter fully off: about +20% top speed (red 35 to 42 mph, blue 38 to 45, yellow 41 to 50), a few hundred more rpm flat out, about 7% more fuel per km. Two clicks lean: red 0 to 20 mph 2.2 to 2.0 s and about 11% less fuel, a little rougher. Checked: no stalls from five hard stops at the lowest idle, leanest mixture and softest clutch on any quad, no creep at the highest idle with the softest clutch.
- Mixture is a new engine param, `mixtureTrim`, so lean and rich running come from the existing engine, smoke and fouling models, with small drive, fuel and warm-up effects on top. Three clicks rich slowly fouls the plug (a stall after roughly 14 minutes' running); the panel says so.
- She reacts when you tinker, take the limiter off, put it back to stock, or ride her on a de-restricted quad faster than it used to go.

## A proper mechanic's day (#24, was TODO 129)

Agreed with Jamie: a guided checklist rather than one long job, only the steps that fit the quad, a clean run makes it smoother, Amy ticks it off, no random "needs a service" fault; and a quad in a bad way also goes on the chalkboard as a job for the day.

- **The checklist** (`src/workshop/service.js`, pure, `tests/service.test.js`): `S` on the bench menu runs `servicePlan(facts)` in order, reusing the existing bench jobs and their botch rules: plug, air filter, oil (two-stroke pre-mix) or sump (petrol four-stroke), then chain, or belt where there is neither a chain nor a manual clutch (the tractor and Land Rovers have no belt job). A diesel gets filter and belt. A `ServiceRun` per unit survives stepping away.
- **Two new jobs**, `filter` and `belt` in `benchJobs.js`, are in `JOBS` but not `JOB_KINDS`: the menu's sixteen keys are all taken, so they are done only inside a service. A badly blown filter leaves `filterLeft` 0.3 clog; a belt with the wrong tension leaves `botchedLevel` 0.2.
- **The payoff.** A finished service with nothing botched takes `serviceSmooth` 0.05 off `baseRoughness`, never below the quad's `roughnessRange[0]`, so each quad keeps its character. A botched step still does that job but the smoothness is lost. The toolbox's V service is unchanged (wear only).
- **Amy.** The logbook gets "full service done, ticked off". On opening the bench on a quad with wear at `bookWear` 0.5, `serviceDue` (Amy nags, Jess heckles); on finishing, `serviceDone`. New lines in `benchLines.js`, recorded.
- **The board.** `setUpServiceJob` in `game.js` asks `pickServiceJob` once at the start of a game: the quad with the most wear past `bookWear` gets a full service; otherwise the one with the most things due (at least two) gets a part service naming them; it goes up `boardChance` 0.7 of the time, from its own stream off the seed. It is added on top of `jobsPerDay`, so the muck and silage still appear. It ticks when every kind it names has been done well on that quad at the bench, by the service or one job at a time (`serviceJobNoted`), so a single botched step stays outstanding. A quad's state is read once at the start, so a service is never posted mid-game.
- **Debug.** Tune: "Service" (all the thresholds). Console: `farm.service`.
- **Left out:** a gamepad button for `S`, a service posted later in a game as quads wear, and a belt job for the tractor and Land Rovers. Checked: unit tests. Not checked in the running game: how the S row and the checklist read in the bench card, the board with three lines, and whether `serviceSmooth` 0.05 is audible.

Done right clears the fault; botched brings it back at `botchedLevel`. The pressures job's window decides the pressure it leaves (right is stock, short or over `pressureOff` out). The tractor's own throttle, and so its drive, is not touched. Not tuned by ear.
- **The model** (`tractorModel.js`: `SPLITTER`, `poseLogSplitter`). A `logSplitter` group, hidden until fitted: a frame from the lower links, a red beam 1.4 m out of the back at 0.95 m, a black hydraulic cylinder over it with a chrome rod to the ram's plate, hoses to the tractor and a triangular wedge at the far end. ## Winching between vehicles (#40, was TODO 145)

- **Anchors** (`src/items/winchAnchor.js`, pure, tested). `pickTree` is a look ray against each trunk as a vertical cylinder (a quarter of a metre forgiving, within 6 m, to 4 m up); `trunkHookPoint` puts the hook on the side facing the green, 0.6 m up; `isHeavy` says which vehicles are never dragged. - **Two ways to pull.** A light quad is dragged to the green as before. - **The rope.** A five-sided tube of 11 mm radius rewritten each frame (18 points), in place of the one-pixel line. ## A booster pack (#58, was TODO 163)

- A black and yellow jump-start pack lies on the back edge of the workbench (`layout.benchPack`). Hold `E` on a switched-off vehicle with it in your hands and it pushes charge into that battery at 0.12 a second, to 0.6 (`boostTo`), so about five seconds from flat. No second vehicle, and the leads are untouched.
- It can run flat: each 1 of battery given costs 1.4 of pack (`drainPerCharge`), so a full pack starts about one and a half flat batteries, and it loses 0.06 overnight (`nightDrain`). Below 0.02 it gives nothing and the toast says to charge it.
- It is charged on the bench charger by the same rules as a battery (generator running, about five minutes). To get that for nothing it lives in the game's `batteries` map under `BOOSTER_ID`, with an `engine`-shaped `{ battery }`, so `heldBattery`, `pickUpBattery`, `putOnCharger`, the charger's lamps, the map and the `G` put-down treat it like any battery. The vehicle-only paths (`fitHeldBattery`, `batteryPrompt`, the bench's fit job, `captureFarm`'s `batteries`) check `BOOSTER_ID` first. It is carried in the UTV's bed and the tub like a battery, and goes back on the bench with `E`.
- Kept in the save as `boosterPack: { charge, where }`, additive, so old saves load. In the morning it is back on the bench, less the night's drain, or still on the charger and full.
- Refused with a toast: a running or cranking vehicle, one with its battery out, one already at 0.6 or more, and a flat pack. The girls neither use it nor react to it (a boosted start is an ordinary engine start to them).
- Debug: Tune "Booster pack"; the Girls tab's workshop folder shows it and has a charge slider; the console's `farm.boosterPack` (`now`, `charge`).

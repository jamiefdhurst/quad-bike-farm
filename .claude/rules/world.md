# The world: the day, lights and things in numbers

- The day moves on (`src/world/daylight.js`): the temperature, the frost and the light change over a game, so read `weather.temperature`, `weather.frost` and `daylight.darkness` as they are now, never once at the start. An effect drawn with its own fixed light colour (a custom shader, a sprite) gets dimmed with the daylight in `updateWeather`, or it glows at night.
- A new light source goes through the fixed pool in `effects/vehicleLights.js` (or a light made at the start and only dimmed), never a light added mid-game, which recompiles every shader.
- Anything scattered over the farm in numbers (plants, stones, posts) goes through `tiledInstances` (`src/world/tiledInstances.js`) or `instanceModel`, never one InstancedMesh for the lot, which is never culled; small ground cover also registers with the draw distance (`drawDistance.add`). docs/decisions/world.md, "Drawing less (frame time)".
- The farm's oaks and bushes (`models.treeA`, `treeB`, `bush`) are handed to `c.scatter.oaks(models, [a, b])` or `c.scatter.bushes(models, matrices)` (`src/world/scatter.js`), never `instanceModel`'d by a builder: they are scattered once with every other builder's, a tile one batch a model, and swapped for the far oak and far bush beyond `treeFullDistance`. One scattered on its own is the full model at any distance. Only a set piece hidden by distance as a whole (the quarry's `quarrySite`) keeps its own. docs/decisions/performance.md, "Oaks and bushes far away (#837)".
- A new set piece (a yard, a group of buildings), a barn fixture or a new kind of vehicle model is named in `src/world/farDetail.js` (`SET_PIECE_NAMES`, `BARN_PROP_NAMES`, `VEHICLE_NAMES`, matching its `group.name`), or it is drawn at any distance. docs/decisions/performance.md, "Draw less far away (#365)". A big one (the village, the neighbour's farm) also gets a cheap far version swapped in by distance, so it never pops out: build it with `buildFarVersion` (`src/world/farVersion.js`) and hang it on the full group's `userData.farVersion`; docs/decisions/performance.md, "A far version of the village and the neighbour's farm (#400)".
- Any small mesh in the scene is left out automatically while it would span under a point on the screen (`FarDetail.leaveOutSpecks`, by moving it to layer 1, never touching `visible`): don't put anything on layer 1, and mark a mesh that must always show, however far, `userData.keepFar` (lights and glows are kept already). docs/decisions/performance.md, "Parts too small to see (#451)".
- After anything that adds to the world or to every frame's work, run `node tools/perf.mjs` (see `checking.md`). CI also counts draw calls and triangles from fixed spots (the smoke test, `tools/frameBudget.mjs`) and prints each with its change from main in the Smoke job's summary: anything added to the world says what it changed there in the PR (in chat if the PR body has no place for it), and a budget is raised only on purpose, with a line in `docs/decisions/performance.md` saying why.
- A position inside a building or a yard is written as an offset from its anchor in `layout.js` (`at('barn', { x, z })`, `anchors` at its head), never as an absolute number, so the building can move as one (`src/world/layoutAnchors.js`). A new thing in or on one goes inside its `at(...)`; the layout test moves and turns every anchor and fails if a number didn't follow. An anchor with a non-zero `yaw` throws unless it says `turnable: true`, which is set only once that piece's builders draw it turned; an issue that needs a turn does that work first. docs/decisions/world.md, "Placing a building's contents from the building (#661)".
- Many small meshes that never move against what they belong to (a vehicle's parts, the barn's fittings, a pen's rails) are merged by material while they hold still (`src/world/staticMerge.js`, run by `FarDetail` over the roots in `MERGED_NAMES` in `farDetail.js`): a new kind of vehicle or building with its own scene child goes in `MERGED_NAMES`, nothing is put on layer 2 (the merged-away originals' layer, as layer 1 is the specks'), and a part that must be drawn by itself (a lamp's glow) is emissive or named `lens`. docs/decisions/performance.md, "Merging what stands still (#816)".
- **Anything placed on the farm joins the list** (`farmPlacements` in `src/world/farmPlacement.js`, #1187): a new builder, or a new thing a builder stands, works its places out as numbers outside the drawing code (a pure function the builder calls, as `layHedge`, `layLaneHedge` and `farmTreeSpots` are) and adds them as a source there, or names itself in `PLACEMENT_SOURCES.notStanding` with why; `tests/farmPlacement.test.js` fails on a builder in `buildProps` that is neither. Ask `placementBreaks` before placing. A new kind of placement bug becomes a rule in that file, run over the whole farm, rather than only a fix where it was seen; what a rule finds that is bigger than a position or a margin is filed and listed in the test's `KNOWN` by its issue. docs/decisions/world.md, "One test for things in the wrong place (#1187)".

    Reference Every vehicle as it is now in the game
    • StartingKey on (three seconds of glow plugs on a diesel), the starter held until it catches, or a kick every 1.5 s on the kick-start bikes, then four seconds of tickover.
    • IdleSix seconds of warm tickover at the middle of the vehicle's roughness range.
    • Rough idleThe same at the top of its range plus 0.3: a neglected one that badly wants a service.
    • RevvingStood still: a small blip, a sharp full blip, then full throttle held for three seconds and let go.
    • DrivingScripted, with no physics: pull away, change up through the gears (or the CVT's launch load), cruise, lift off in gear, clutch in and stop.
